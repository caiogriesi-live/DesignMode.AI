# Marketing Performance Dashboard

## Objective

Deliver an authenticated internal dashboard for company-level Google Ads performance, Stripe cash collections, manually recorded business expenses, and financial measures. Use the approved business definitions and reporting boundaries so company users can distinguish marketing profitability from overall business profitability.

## Current State

- `DesignMode.AI.Client/Pages/Dashboard.razor` implements `/init/dashboard` as a project list with create/open project actions. `RootDashboard.razor` routes authenticated users from `/dashboard` to that component. Neither page currently presents marketing or financial metrics.
- `DesignMode.AI.Server/DashboardProjectsFunction.cs` and `Services/DashboardPromptQuery.cs` provide an authenticated, user-scoped projects API backed by DynamoDB prompt data. This is a project listing, not an analytics data source.
- Stripe purchase measurement exists in `Services/PurchaseMeasurementStore.cs` and `PurchaseMeasurementModels.cs`. It records verified paid transactions in DynamoDB `Purchases`, including amount, currency, paid time, billing reason, first-payment/renewal flags, user, project, funnel, and checkout attribution snapshot. Purchase writes are connected to verified Stripe webhooks and protected production flows.
- Attribution infrastructure exists in `AttributionStore`, `AttributionFunctions`, checkout snapshots, and related models. It captures acquisition and Google click identifiers and records consent context. `GoogleConversionDelivery.cs` supports Google Analytics 4 measurement and contains legacy Google Ads click-conversion upload code, but `PurchaseConversionDispatcher` dispatches GA4 only; that upload path is not Google Ads reporting or spend ingestion.
- No Google Ads reporting client, campaign spend data model, business expense ledger, or marketing financial metrics API/UI was found in the application source. No direct expense entry/import workflow was found.
- Existing tests cover project dashboard behavior and purchase measurement/attribution behavior; no financial dashboard tests or Google Ads reporting/expense tests were found.
- Google Ads OAuth-related environment setting names are present for the legacy conversion upload integration. No evidence was found of a reporting connection or account ownership/configuration workflow. No credentials were inspected or copied into this plan.

## Desired Outcome

The authenticated company dashboard presents date-filterable USD reporting in the `America/New_York` reporting timezone with calendar-day boundaries. It shows Google Ads spend and Google-attributed performance, collected Stripe subscription revenue (initial payments and renewals), one-time AI credit purchases separately, refunds and chargebacks, Stripe fees, manual business expenses, and appropriate profitability measures. It distinguishes Google-attributed from unattributed customers and revenue, displays total revenue separately from Google-attributed revenue, identifies non-USD records as non-comparable without converting them, and communicates source freshness and incomplete data.

## Scope

- Extend the existing dashboard and server architecture for company-level financial marketing reporting.
- Integrate the company's Google Ads account for read-only reporting. Preserve existing Google conversion-upload and measurement behavior.
- Reuse canonical Stripe purchase measurement records as a reporting input. Include initial subscription payments and renewals in subscription revenue; present one-time AI credit purchases separately.
- Subtract refunds and chargebacks from revenue. Exclude taxes from revenue. Present Stripe fees separately and subtract them in net profit calculations.
- Provide manual expense entry in DesignMode.AI with date, amount, category, and description/vendor. Initial categories: software, AI/API, hosting/infrastructure, contractors, payroll, advertising/marketing, professional services, and other. Authorized authenticated company users may add and edit records.
- Report collected revenue less refunds, Stripe fees, Google Ads spend, and approved business expenses as overall business profit. Also distinguish marketing profitability from overall business profitability where appropriate.
- Calculate CAC as Google Ads spend divided by first-time paying customers attributed to Google Ads, using existing captured Google click/acquisition attribution associated with acquisition and checkout for the initial version. Report unattributed customers separately; do not invent multi-touch attribution.
- Calculate ROAS from Google-attributed revenue divided by Google Ads spend; display primarily as a multiple such as 3.4x. Show total revenue separately.
- Support Today, last 7 days, last 30 days, month-to-date, previous month, year-to-date, and custom range. Where meaningful, compare the selected period with the immediately preceding equivalent period.
- Add focused automated coverage for aggregation, access boundaries, and UI behavior during later engineering work.

## Out of Scope

- Changes to Stripe checkout, webhooks, subscription lifecycle, payment processing, purchase ledger writes, refunds processing, or production financial transactions.
- Changes to authentication/authorization mechanisms, attribution capture/consent policy, AI usage accounting, or production infrastructure/deployment configuration.
- Changes to existing Google conversion-upload or measurement behavior; Google Ads campaign management or write access.
- Multi-touch attribution, invented attribution windows/rules, or assigning unattributed customers to Google.
- Automated bookkeeping, tax/accounting statements, forecasting, budget optimization, or external accounting integration.
- Currency conversion of non-USD records until an exchange-rate policy is approved; such records are reported separately as non-comparable.
- Historical data mutation or backfill beyond read-only reporting over available approved source records.

## Architecture and Existing Systems

- Use the current Blazor WebAssembly client, Azure Functions isolated-worker API, shared contracts, and DynamoDB persistence patterns. The existing project list is the starting dashboard surface; keep its project query separate from financial aggregation.
- Treat verified `Purchases` measurement records as a Stripe reporting source rather than querying payment-processing APIs from the dashboard. Reporting reads must not expose payment identifiers or unrelated users' records. Confirm which available records represent refunds, chargebacks, taxes, and fees without changing source writes.
- Implement Google Ads reporting as a separate read-only integration against the single company-level account. Existing conversion-upload configuration is not evidence of reporting authorization and must not be repurposed in a way that changes measurement behavior.
- Implement an in-application manual expense ledger; there is no existing expense system to extend. Restrict add/edit/read access to authorized authenticated company users.
- Keep reporting calculations outside protected payment transaction paths. Do not store raw secrets in client code or return them to clients.
- Use USD as the comparable reporting currency and `America/New_York` as the calendar-day reporting boundary. Clearly separate non-USD records from comparable totals and ratios.

## Milestones

### Milestone 1: Establish reporting contracts and source read paths

Define shared reporting contracts from the approved definitions and implement read-only paths for Google Ads and Stripe sources, plus the manual expense ledger and authorized access.

### Milestone 2: Deliver financial aggregation and dashboard experience

Calculate approved metrics and comparisons for supported date ranges, and present them in the authenticated dashboard with clear source, freshness, currency, and data-quality states.

### Milestone 3: Verify reporting correctness and access boundaries

Cover metric definitions and edge cases, user/company scoping, and UI states. Validate connected-source reporting in an authorized read-only environment without triggering transactions or conversion uploads.

## Risks

- The inspected purchase model records verified paid transactions, but source availability and representation of refunds, chargebacks, taxes, and Stripe fees must be verified before metric completeness can be claimed. Missing inputs must be shown as unavailable or incomplete rather than silently treated as zero.
- Existing acquisition snapshots and Google click identifiers support the approved initial attribution basis but may not associate every first-time customer with Google. Unattributed customers must remain separate; do not infer multi-touch attribution.
- Google Ads reporting requires a reporting-authorized connection to the company account. The legacy conversion-upload configuration is for a different purpose and must not be changed by this work.
- Non-USD values cannot be compared with USD until an exchange-rate policy is approved; they must remain visibly separate.
- User/company-scoped access and aggregation must avoid cross-account leakage while maintaining acceptable DynamoDB query cost and latency.
- Manual expense records depend on authorized users entering accurate, timely data; the dashboard should communicate its recorded-data basis.

## Protected Systems

- Authentication and authorization must remain unchanged except for applying the existing authenticated boundary and approved company-user authorization to reporting and expense operations.
- Stripe checkout, webhooks, subscriptions, production financial transactions, and canonical purchase ledger writes are protected and excluded from modification.
- Google OAuth credentials and external account permissions are protected; use only the company account's approved minimum read-only reporting access and never expose secrets to the client.
- Production databases and financial records are read-only inputs except for the new, separately scoped manual expense records; no historical source-data mutation is authorized.
- Attribution and consent systems are protected; existing attribution data may be read under the approved initial Google click/acquisition basis without changing collection or advertising measurement behavior.
- Deployment configuration, secrets, AI usage accounting, and AIGenerationUsage are out of scope.

## Definition of Done

- The authenticated company dashboard presents real data from available approved sources for each supported date range, with meaningful preceding-period comparisons where applicable.
- Revenue, refunds/chargebacks, taxes, Stripe fees, expenses, overall business profit, marketing profitability, CAC, and ROAS follow the approved definitions and are clearly labeled.
- Subscription initial payments and renewals are included in subscription revenue; one-time AI credit purchases are shown separately. Total revenue and Google-attributed revenue are distinct, and unattributed customers are reported separately.
- Non-USD records are shown as non-comparable and are not silently converted or included in comparable USD metrics.
- Authorized authenticated company users can add and edit expenses with the approved fields and categories.
- Reporting APIs enforce the company authorization boundary and expose no secrets or unrelated companies' financial details.
- Automated tests cover approved calculation rules, material edge cases, partial/source failure states, expense authorization, and access boundaries.
- Stripe transaction, subscription, webhook, authentication, attribution-consent, existing Google conversion-upload, and production source-data write behavior remain unchanged.
