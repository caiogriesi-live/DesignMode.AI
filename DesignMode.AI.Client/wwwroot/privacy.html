Email Communications

After you create an account or sign up, we may use the email address associated with your account to send communications about your DesignMode experience, including account emails such as requested password resets.

If you begin but do not complete the onboarding or design process, we may email you when your generated design preview is ready. This status notification may also encourage you to return, view your preview, and continue your unfinished project. Not every generation results in an email.

Additional follow-up reminders encouraging you to return to an unfinished project are promotional or lifecycle marketing communications. We may send these reminders for eligible unfinished projects unless you have opted out of recovery reminders. They are designed to stop when you subscribe or complete the relevant onboarding or design process.

You can opt out of promotional recovery reminders for all projects associated with your account by following the unsubscribe link in a recovery email and confirming your choice on the unsubscribe page. This choice does not stop service emails, such as preview-ready notifications or requested password resets. Communication practices are subject to applicable consent and opt-out requirements; creating an account or reading this policy does not replace affirmative marketing consent where required.

Advertising measurement

For new activity after this update is deployed, DesignMode internal advertising measurement and Google advertising measurement are enabled. We process attribution to understand how users reached DesignMode, measure advertising campaign performance, attribute purchases and conversions, prevent duplicate conversion reporting, and improve marketing effectiveness.

Identifiers may include Google click identifiers (gclid, gbraid, wbraid), UTM and campaign parameters, browser analytics identifiers when analytics is enabled, and purchase metadata such as value, currency, timestamp, and a transaction identifier. Google advertising storage and advertising user-data signals are enabled; ad personalization remains disabled. Google Ads conversion delivery is not enabled in this release.

We do not intentionally send prompts, generated creative content, Stripe secret or payment credentials, or internal AI-generation data to Google analytics or advertising measurement. We do not send email or phone as Ads conversion identifiers; a future feature changing that practice would require an updated disclosure.

Analytics preference

Privacy-filtered analytics is enabled by default. You can disable or re-enable analytics for this browser independently of the always-enabled advertising-measurement policy.
